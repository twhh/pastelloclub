# Pinterest Launch List

Launch plan. Pins live in `public/images/posts/pins/` (and covers in
`posts/` double as pins where noted). Schedule via Pinterest's native scheduler.

## One-time setup (~1 hour)

1. Create a business account at pinterest.com/business (converts or fresh - use the site email)
2. Claim `pastelloclub.com`: Settings > Claimed accounts (enables Rich Pins: title/description auto-attach to links)
3. Create the boards, in this order:
   - **Trump Accounts for Parents** - description: "How the new Trump Account works for families: claiming the $1,000, contribution rules, and what happens at 18. Compared against 529s, UTMAs, and custodial Roths - a dad claiming one for his infant, with the numbers."
   - **Baby Money Tips** - description: "Money math for self-employed parents: 529s, Solo 401(k), quarterly taxes, and the Trump Account's free $1,000. Real contribution logs and returns from five years of running a small business."
   - **Baby Gear That Lasts** - description: "Gear after four years and two kids: what held up, what broke, and whether it earns its shelf space. Strollers, monitors, wagons - all bought, none free."
   - **Toddler Laughs** (new, Nov 2026) - description: "Knock knock jokes, clean jokes, animal jokes, nursery rhymes, and name lists for toddlers and preschoolers - the screen-free stuff that actually lands with a two-year-old. Tested on a real four-year-old."
4. Profile: name "pastelloclub", bio "Gear that lasts (four years, two kids), money math for self-employed parents, and Trump Account guides - Bay Area dad of two.", link pastelloclub.com

## Pin rules

- Always pin the deep URL (the post), never the homepage
- Title = benefit phrase; description = 2-3 keyword sentences + a verb ("See the full walkthrough")
- Fresh pins to the same URL are fine; new image beats re-pin
- Pin at the post's publish moment when dates align (release + pin same day)
- Alt text (under advanced options when creating the pin): describe what the graphic shows + keyword, under ~100 chars - see tables
