# Pinterest Launch List

Two-week starter plan. Pins live in `public/images/posts/pins/` (and covers in
`posts/` double as pins where noted). Schedule via Pinterest's native scheduler.

## One-time setup (~1 hour)

1. Create a business account at pinterest.com/business (converts or fresh - use the site email)
2. Claim `pastelloclub.com`: Settings > Claimed accounts (enables Rich Pins: title/description auto-attach to links)
3. Create three boards, in this order:
   - **Trump Accounts for Parents** - description: "How the new Trump Account works for families: claiming the $1,000, contribution rules, and what happens at 18. Honest notes from a family of four."
   - **Baby Money Tips** - description: "Money notes for parents: 529s, savings, and budgeting on a variable income. Real numbers, no lectures."
   - **Honest Baby Gear Reviews** - description: "Baby gear after years of real use, not a showroom visit. Strollers, monitors, wagons - what held up and what broke."
4. Profile: name "pastelloclub", bio "Honest baby gear reviews, money notes for parents, and Trump Account guides - from a family of two kids, one dog, and one small business.", link pastelloclub.com

## Pin rules

- Always pin the deep URL (the post), never the homepage
- Title = benefit phrase; description = 2-3 keyword sentences + a verb ("See the full walkthrough")
- Fresh pins to the same URL are fine; new image beats re-pin
- Pin at the post's publish moment when dates align (release + pin same day)
