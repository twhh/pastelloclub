---
title: "On the long minutes of bedtime"
description: "Bedtime is not a finish line. It is a small ritual, repeated."
pubDate: 2026-05-19
tags: ["sleep", "rituals"]
---

There is a particular shape to the last twenty minutes of a small child's day.
It bends. It stretches. It pretends to end, then doesn't.

The trick — if there is a trick — is to stop treating it as a finish line.
Bedtime is a doorway, and doorways take a moment.

Some nights that means three books instead of one. Some nights it means sitting
in the dark listening to a tiny voice narrate the entire plot of *Bluey*.
None of it is wasted. All of it is the thing.

When the door finally closes, the house exhales. Stand there for a second.
You did something today.
