Every registry guide treats the baby registry as a gift ask: what to list so other people buy it for you. That framing misses the version that actually pays. If you already shop on Amazon, the registry is a discount program wearing a gift list as a costume.

We built ours mostly for us. Here's everything it pays, in the order it pays you.

## What You Actually Get

Three discounts and a bonus box:

- **The welcome box.** Once your registry exists and you've bought a small amount from it yourself (about $10 at last check), Amazon sends [a welcome box](https://www.amazon.com/b?ie=UTF8&node=21499367011) - samples, bottles, coupons, the occasional full-size surprise. It is free stuff for making a list you needed anyway.
- **Registry discounts on listed items**, on top of normal Amazon pricing, through the life of the registry.
- **The 15% completion discount.** As the due date closes in, whatever's still unpurchased on your list gets [a completion discount](https://www.amazon.com/baby-reg?ref_=nav_cs_baby_registry) - 15% off the stragglers. This is the big one: the trick is *letting* the big-ticket items sit unpurchased until that window, then buying them from yourself.

Terms shift season to season, so treat those numbers as the shape of the deal rather than a quote.

## The Checklist That Does the Planning

The sleeper feature is the registry itself as a planning tool. Amazon's categories - diapering, feeding, strollers, car seats, activity gear, nursing, furniture, bedding, bathing, toys, clothing - are a complete taxonomy of a first-year baby. We used it as the master checklist, including for things we ended up buying at Costco or Target. Everything needing a decision got on one list; nothing got discovered in month three in a panic.

If you're the kind of parent building spreadsheets, this kills the spreadsheet.

## The Stack

This is where it compounds with the rest of the money math:

1. **Registry completion discount** on the big gear (stroller, car seat, furniture) - [the Cruz-vs-Vista post](/posts/cruz-vs-vista-choice/) covers why we'd still buy the cheaper stroller, discount or not.
2. **Diapers through the Pampers app** - the rewards stack on sale prices on top of the registry discount on top of subscribe-and-save. [The whole system is here](/posts/pampers-rewards/).
3. **The unclaimed box** as the cherry: free samples for ten minutes of list-making.

None of these requires spending a dollar you weren't already going to spend. That's the entire point.

## If You Take One Thing

[Make the registry](https://www.amazon.com/baby-reg?ref_=nav_cs_baby_registry) today, list what you were buying anyway, and let the discounts, the checklist, and the box arrive on their own schedule. A registry costs nothing to start - the only failure mode is building it at a baby shower instead of at the beginning, when the 15% still has big-ticket items to work on.
