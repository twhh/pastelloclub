Welcome to **pastelloclub** — a quiet, slow blog for parents.

We started it because the internet got loud, and parenting got louder, and we wanted
a small room where the lights are warm and nobody is selling anything.

Here you'll find short pieces on the everyday stuff:

- the long minutes of bedtime
- snacks that survived the airport
- how to apologize to a three-year-old
- why "boring" Saturdays are the best Saturdays

> If you read one thing here and feel a little softer afterward, we did our job.

— pastelloclub
