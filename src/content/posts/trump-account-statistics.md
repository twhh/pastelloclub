The Trump Account program is generating real numbers now, and most of the ones flying around social media are wrong. This page collects the figures worth citing, each with its source, and gets refreshed as new data lands. Last updated October 2026.

## The Numbers

**About 4 million children were signed up before automatic enrollment began.** Roughly 4 million by mid-2026, per the IRS (release IR-2026-68), through the portal or Form 4547 attached to tax returns.

**Automatic enrollment is complete, and it covered more than 60 million children.** Per the [Treasury's October 1, 2026 announcement](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0642/), every eligible child under 18 with a valid Social Security number now has a Trump Account - over 60 million accounts opened administratively, on top of the millions who enrolled themselves. Auto-opened is not the same as claimed: parents still activate and elect the $1,000.

**Fewer than 1 in 12 eligible families claimed the $1,000 under the old paper process.** During the first tax-filing season, the IRS reported that only a small fraction of eligible families attached the election to their returns. That failure rate is why auto-enrollment exists.

**The seed costs the government about $1,000 per child, once, for four birth years.** Children born January 1, 2025 through December 31, 2028 qualify - per the IRS - if they're U.S. citizens with SSNs. No contribution from the family is required to get it.

**Families can add up to $5,000 per child per year, and going over costs you.** Per the Congressional Research Service, contributions past the cap trigger excise penalties - excess contributions aren't just disallowed, they're penalized.

**During the growth period, an auto-account can only accept regular contributions and the $1,000 pilot deposit.** Per the adopted IRS regulations (Thomson Reuters has the [regulation summary](https://tax.thomsonreuters.com/news/irs-adopts-trump-account-auto-enrollment-reproposes-rules/)) - no rollovers or other funding sources while the account grows.

**At 18, the account automatically becomes a Traditional IRA, with a Roth election available.** Per U.S. Bank's program guide and the statute. [We explain what that means in practice here](/posts/trump-account-what-happens-at-18/).

## What Nobody Can Cite Yet

The number that matters most - **how many of the 60 million auto-opened accounts have actually been activated and had the $1,000 elected** - has not been published. Treasury has not released claims-paid data. When it does, this section gets a real number and the claim-rate gap stops being an estimate.

## Why the Gap Is the Story

Roughly 4 million families did the paperwork on their own. The program's own response to the other tens of millions was to stop waiting: auto-open everything and make claiming a twenty-minute task. The [claim walkthrough](/posts/trump-account-claim-guide/) is that twenty minutes, and [the scam wave riding it](/posts/trump-account-email-scams/) is the reason to type the .gov address yourself.

If you're deciding what the $1,000 is worth before claiming it, [the calculator](/tools/trump-account-calculator/) projects it to 18, 21, and 30 - with or without adding anything yourself.
